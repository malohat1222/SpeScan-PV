# SpeScan research and product roadmap

**Pre-MVP** — stages below distinguish existing repository assets from proposed work.

| Stage | Milestone | Status | Demonstration required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ESP32 firmware scaffold, serial control, INA219 acquisition, CSV output | Existing repository baseline (not independently hardware-tested here) | Reproducible software build and demonstration |
| 2 | Reflective-grating optical layout and lamp safety design | Design / integration | Model, alignment and safe enclosed optical setup |
| 3 | Independently controlled lower and upper spectral-band edges | Planned | Calibrated two-edge slit with repeatable λ1, λ2 |
| 4 | Calibrated sample-plane optical power and cell temperature | Planned | Reference calibration and synchronized data collection |
| 5 | Controlled electrical-load sweep, full I–V curve and MPP extraction | Planned | Accuracy comparison to reference I–V measurements |
| 6 | Separate Si, Ge, GaAs material studies | Planned | Replicated experimental series; Ge range extension assessment |
| 7 | Material-specific spectral optimization | Planned | Transparent optimization criteria and uncertainty |
| 8 | Selective filter design and thermal–electrical validation | Long-term research | Filter/no-filter comparison under controlled conditions |
| 9 | Product pilot testing, market and pricing validation | Future business development | Interviews, quotations and pilot evidence |

Do not change a `Planned` status to `Completed` until a dated and reproducible result is available.
